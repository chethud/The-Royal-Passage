## A. General Payment Policy

---

## A1. Introduction

This Payment, Refund & Cancellation Policy ("Policy") explains how payments, cancellations, refunds, and related financial transactions are handled on **The Royal Passage** ("Platform", "Company", "we", "our", or "us").

This Policy applies to all bookings made through The Royal Passage, including:

- Experience Bookings
- Homestay Bookings
- VIP Packages
- Custom VIP Travel Requests
- Any additional travel-related services introduced by the Platform.

By making a booking through The Royal Passage, you agree to comply with this Policy, our Terms & Conditions, and our Privacy Policy.

---

## A2. Scope

This Policy governs:

- Payment methods
- Booking confirmation
- Platform service fees
- Cancellation charges
- Refund eligibility
- Refund timelines
- Payment failures
- Duplicate transactions
- Chargebacks
- Technical issues
- Fraud prevention
- Customer support for payment-related matters

Where a specific Experience, Homestay, or VIP Package contains additional payment terms, those terms will apply in addition to this Policy.

---

## A3. Accepted Payment Methods

All bookings on The Royal Passage must be paid online through our supported payment gateway.

Currently accepted payment methods include:

- UPI
- Credit Cards
- Debit Cards
- Net Banking

Cash payments, offline transfers, or direct payments to Experience Hosts, Homestay Owners, or VIP Providers are **not permitted**, unless expressly authorized by The Royal Passage.

---

## A4. Payment Gateway

The Royal Passage currently uses **Razorpay** to securely process online payments.

All payment transactions are subject to Razorpay's own terms, security standards, and processing timelines.

The Royal Passage does not store your complete payment credentials, including:

- Credit or Debit Card Numbers
- CVV
- UPI PIN
- Net Banking Passwords
- One-Time Passwords (OTP)

---

## A5. Currency

All prices displayed on the Platform are shown in **Indian Rupees (INR)** unless otherwise specified.

If your payment method uses another currency, your bank or payment provider may apply:

- Currency conversion charges
- Foreign exchange rates
- International transaction fees

These charges are determined by your financial institution and are not controlled by The Royal Passage.

---

## A6. Booking Confirmation

A booking is considered confirmed only after:

1. Full payment has been successfully processed.
2. Payment verification has been completed (where applicable).
3. A Booking Confirmation and Booking ID have been issued.

Until these conditions are met, no reservation is guaranteed.

---

## A7. Platform Service Fee

The Royal Passage charges a **10% Platform Service Fee** on every confirmed booking for Experiences, Homestays, and VIP Services.

This fee supports:

- Platform operations
- Technology infrastructure
- Secure payment processing
- Customer support
- Booking management
- Fraud prevention
- Marketing and promotional activities
- Continuous platform improvements

The Platform Service Fee is displayed during checkout before payment is completed.

### A7.1 Non-Refundable Platform Service Fee

Unless otherwise required by applicable law, or where the booking is cancelled by the Host, Property Owner, VIP Provider, or due to a verified Platform error, the **10% Platform Service Fee is non-refundable**, even if the booking is later cancelled by the customer.

---

## A8. Taxes

Applicable taxes, including GST or other government-imposed taxes and fees, will be calculated and displayed during checkout where required by law.

Users are responsible for paying all applicable taxes included in the booking amount.

---

## A9. Pricing

Prices displayed on the Platform are determined by the respective Experience Host, Homestay Owner, or VIP Provider.

Prices may vary due to:

- Seasonal demand
- Weekends
- Public holidays
- Festivals
- Availability
- Promotional offers
- Group size
- Custom requests

The Royal Passage reserves the right to correct obvious pricing errors before confirming a booking.

---

## A10. Payment Security

The Royal Passage is committed to protecting your payment information.

We implement reasonable security measures, including:

- SSL/TLS encryption for data transmission
- Secure integration with Razorpay
- Encrypted storage of transaction references (not sensitive payment credentials)
- Fraud detection systems
- Continuous monitoring for suspicious payment activity

Users are responsible for ensuring they access the Platform from secure devices and do not share payment credentials with anyone.

---

## B. Experience Payment Policy

---

## B1. Experience Booking Payment

All Experience bookings made through The Royal Passage require **100% advance payment** at the time of booking.

A booking is considered confirmed only after:

- Successful payment through the Platform.
- Payment verification (if applicable).
- Generation of a Booking ID.
- Confirmation email and/or SMS is sent to the Guest.

Until these conditions are fulfilled, no reservation or seat is guaranteed.

---

## B2. Accepted Payment Methods

Payments for Experiences may be made using:

- UPI
- Credit Cards
- Debit Cards
- Net Banking

Payments made outside The Royal Passage Platform are strictly prohibited unless expressly authorized by The Royal Passage.

The Platform shall not be responsible for payments made directly to Experience Hosts.

---

## B3. Booking Confirmation

Upon successful payment, Guests will receive:

- Booking ID
- Experience Name
- Date and Time
- Meeting Point
- Number of Participants
- Payment Receipt
- Host Information (where applicable)
- Cancellation Policy

Guests should verify all booking details immediately and notify Customer Support of any discrepancies within **24 hours**.

---

## B4. Platform Service Fee

The Royal Passage charges a **10% Platform Service Fee** on every confirmed Experience booking.

The Platform Service Fee covers:

- Secure payment processing
- Booking management
- Customer support
- Platform operations
- Technology infrastructure
- Fraud detection and prevention
- Marketing and promotional activities
- Continuous service improvements

The Platform Service Fee is clearly displayed before payment is completed.

Except where the booking is cancelled by the Experience Host or due to a verified Platform error, the Platform Service Fee is **non-refundable**.

---

## B5. Experience Cancellation by Guest

Guests may cancel a confirmed Experience booking through:

- Their Royal Passage account, or
- By contacting Customer Support.

The cancellation becomes effective only after confirmation by The Royal Passage.

Refund eligibility depends on the time remaining before the Experience and the applicable cancellation schedule below.

---

## B6. Experience Cancellation Schedule

| Time Before Experience | Platform Fee | Cancellation Charge                | Refund to Guest |
| ---------------------- | ------------ | ---------------------------------- | --------------- |
| 15 days or more        | 10% retained | No cancellation charge             | 90% Refund      |
| 14–11 days             | 10% retained | 25% booking charge                 | 65% Refund      |
| 10–8 days              | 10% retained | 50% booking charge                 | 40% Refund      |
| Less than 7 days       | 10% retained | Remaining booking amount forfeited | No Refund       |
| No Show                | 10% retained | Full booking amount forfeited      | No Refund       |

The cancellation timeline is calculated based on the scheduled start time of the Experience.

---

## B7. Experience Rescheduling

Guests may request to reschedule an Experience subject to:

- Host approval.
- Availability on the requested date.
- Request being made at least **72 hours** before the Experience.

Rescheduling is not guaranteed.

Where a higher-priced Experience or date is selected, the Guest must pay the price difference.

If the new booking is lower in price, the difference may not be refunded unless otherwise stated.

---

## B8. Host Cancellation

An Experience Host may cancel a booking only for valid operational or safety reasons, including:

- Medical emergency
- Unsafe weather
- Government restrictions
- Venue closure
- Force Majeure
- Operational emergencies

If the Host cancels:

Guests may choose:

- Full refund (including the 10% Platform Service Fee), or
- Reschedule to another available date, or
- Book another Experience of equal value (subject to availability).

---

## B9. No-Show Policy

A Guest will be considered a **No-Show** if they:

- Fail to arrive at the meeting point.
- Arrive after the Experience has commenced and cannot be accommodated.
- Leave before the Experience begins without notifying the Host.
- Cannot be contacted at the registered phone number.

No refunds, credits, or rescheduling will be provided for No-Show bookings unless otherwise required by applicable law.

---

## B10. Late Arrival

Guests are advised to arrive at least **15–30 minutes** before the scheduled reporting time.

If a Guest arrives late:

- The Host is not obligated to delay the Experience.
- Participation may be denied if late arrival would affect safety or the experience of other participants.
- No refund shall be provided for missed or shortened participation due to late arrival.

---

## B11. Refund Processing

Where a refund is approved:

- Refund approval will generally occur within **2 business days** after verification.
- Refunds will be initiated to the original payment method.
- Payment gateway processing may take **2–5 business days**.
- Banks or financial institutions may take an additional **5–10 business days** to credit the amount.

The Royal Passage is not responsible for delays caused by payment providers or banks after the refund has been initiated.

---

## B12. Non-Refundable Charges

The following amounts are generally non-refundable:

- 10% Platform Service Fee (except where the Host cancels or a verified Platform error occurs)
- Taxes and statutory charges where non-refundable under applicable law
- Optional services already provided
- Processing fees charged by payment providers, where permitted

---

## B13. Experience Modifications

If a Guest requests changes to a confirmed booking, such as:

- Date changes
- Participant count
- Special requests

approval will depend on:

- Host availability
- Operational feasibility
- Time remaining before the Experience

Additional charges may apply where the modified booking has a higher value.

---

## B14. Guest Responsibilities for Refund Claims

To assist with refund investigations, Guests may be required to provide:

- Booking ID
- Payment receipt
- Transaction reference number
- Screenshots (where relevant)
- Any supporting documentation requested by Customer Support

Failure to provide requested information may delay the investigation and refund process.

---

## B15. Exceptional Circumstances

The Royal Passage may, at its sole discretion, consider refunds or alternative arrangements outside the standard cancellation schedule in exceptional situations, including:

- Verified medical emergencies
- Government-imposed travel restrictions
- Natural disasters
- Force Majeure events
- Other circumstances supported by appropriate documentation

Such requests will be assessed on a case-by-case basis and do not create an obligation to provide refunds in similar future situations.

---

- Successful online payment.
- Payment verification (if applicable).
- Booking acceptance by the Property Owner (where approval is required).
- Generation of a Booking ID.
- Confirmation email and/or SMS is issued.

No room or accommodation shall be considered reserved until the booking has been confirmed by The Royal Passage.

---

## C2. Accepted Payment Methods

Homestay payments may be made using the payment methods supported by the Platform, including:

- UPI
- Credit Cards
- Debit Cards
- Net Banking

Guests must not make payments directly to Property Owners unless expressly instructed by The Royal Passage.

The Platform shall not be responsible for any payments made outside the official booking process.

---

## C3. Booking Confirmation

Upon successful confirmation, Guests will receive:

- Booking ID
- Property Name
- Property Address
- Check-in Date
- Check-out Date
- Number of Guests
- Room Details
- Payment Receipt
- Property Owner Contact Information (where applicable)
- House Rules

Guests are responsible for reviewing all booking details and reporting any discrepancies within **24 hours** of receiving the confirmation.

---

## C4. Platform Service Fee

The Royal Passage charges a **10% Platform Service Fee** on every confirmed Homestay booking.

This fee contributes to:

- Platform maintenance
- Booking management
- Customer support
- Secure payment processing
- Technology infrastructure
- Fraud detection
- Marketing and promotional activities
- Continuous platform improvements

The Platform Service Fee is displayed during checkout and is included in the total amount payable.

Except where the Property Owner cancels the booking or where a verified Platform error occurs, the Platform Service Fee is **non-refundable**.

---

## C5. Check-in Policy

Guests must:

- Arrive during the property's published check-in hours unless prior arrangements have been made.
- Present a valid Government-issued Photo ID for every adult guest at check-in, where required by law.
- Comply with any registration requirements imposed by the property or applicable authorities.

Failure to comply may result in refusal of check-in without a refund where permitted by applicable law.

---

## C6. Check-out Policy

Guests must vacate the property by the published check-out time unless an extension has been approved.

Late check-out:

- Is subject to availability.
- May incur additional charges determined by the Property Owner.
- May not be available during peak occupancy periods.

---

## C7. House Rules

Guests agree to comply with the Property Owner's published house rules, which may include restrictions on:

- Smoking
- Alcohol consumption
- Loud music or parties
- Pets
- Additional visitors
- Illegal activities
- Damage to property
- Use of shared facilities

Repeated or serious violations of house rules may result in immediate termination of the stay without refund.

---

## C8. Guest Conduct

Guests shall:

- Respect the property and its furnishings.
- Behave responsibly toward neighbors and staff.
- Maintain cleanliness.
- Follow safety instructions.
- Avoid disturbing other guests.
- Use amenities responsibly.

Guests shall not:

- Engage in unlawful activities.
- Cause intentional damage.
- Tamper with security systems.
- Remove property belonging to the Homestay.
- Use the property for commercial activities without authorization.

---

## C9. Property Damage

Guests are responsible for any damage caused to the property, furnishings, appliances, or amenities due to negligence, misuse, or intentional acts.

Where damage is identified:

- The Property Owner must report it promptly.
- Evidence such as photographs, invoices, or repair estimates may be requested.
- The Royal Passage may assist in facilitating communication between the parties but is not responsible for independently assessing or guaranteeing damage claims.

Guests may be required to compensate the Property Owner for verified damages in accordance with applicable law and the booking terms.

---

## C10. Security Deposit

Certain Homestays may require a refundable security deposit.

Where applicable:

- The amount will be clearly displayed before booking.
- The deposit is intended to cover verified damage or other agreed charges.
- Any deductions should be supported by reasonable evidence.
- The remaining balance, if any, will be refunded after inspection in accordance with the property's stated timeline.

## C11. Guest Cancellation

Guests may cancel a confirmed Homestay booking through:

- Their Royal Passage account, or

## F. Payment Failures

---

## F1. Failed Transactions

If payment fails due to:

- Banking issues
- Payment gateway interruptions
- Network connectivity problems
- User interruption
- Incorrect payment information

the booking will remain incomplete until successful payment is received.

---

## F2. Amount Debited but Booking Not Confirmed

If payment is deducted but no booking confirmation is received:

1. Contact Customer Support within **24 hours**.
2. Provide your Booking Reference (if available), transaction ID, and payment receipt.
3. The Royal Passage will investigate the transaction.

After verification, either:

- the booking will be confirmed, or
- the payment will be refunded to the original payment method.

---

## F3. Duplicate Payments

If more than one payment is made for the same booking:

- Notify The Royal Passage within **7 calendar days**.
- Verified duplicate payments will be refunded to the original payment method.

## G. Chargebacks

Guests are encouraged to contact Customer Support before initiating a chargeback through their bank or card issuer.

If a chargeback is initiated:

- The related booking may be suspended.
- Services may be withheld until the dispute is resolved.
- The Royal Passage may provide transaction records and booking details to the payment provider.
- Fraudulent or abusive chargebacks may result in suspension or termination of the user's account and other action permitted by law.

---

## H. Fraud Prevention

To protect Guests, Hosts, Property Owners, VIP Providers, and the Platform, The Royal Passage may investigate suspected fraudulent activity.

Examples include:

- False identity
- Fake bookings
- Stolen payment methods
- Chargeback abuse
- Forged documents
- Fake reviews
- Multiple fraudulent accounts
- Booking manipulation
- Promotional abuse

Where fraud is reasonably suspected, The Royal Passage may:

- Suspend or cancel bookings.
- Delay or withhold refunds during investigation.
- Request additional identity verification.
- Suspend or terminate user accounts.
- Report suspected unlawful activity to the relevant authorities where required by law.
